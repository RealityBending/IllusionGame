Instructions

On each trial you will see two coloured circles, one on the left and one on the right. Your task is to decide which circle is the largest.

Press ← if the left circle is larger, and → if the right circle is larger.

Tap the left half of the screen if the left circle is larger, and tap the right half if the right circle is larger.

This is a reaction time task: answer as fast as you can while staying accurate. The differences will usually be much smaller than in these examples; go with your first impression rather than scrutinising the circles.

There are trials in short blocks, with a break between blocks. Keep both eyes open and stay in full screen. On a phone, hold it in landscape for a larger display.
